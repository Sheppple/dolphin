Four pages around Miles

A page-by-page plan for Cocoon built around your Spider-Verse wallpaper. Widgets come in any rectangle of squares; games, folders and apps take exactly one square. Every page leaves Miles uncovered and puts its header GIF on the ledge below him.

The nav banners Cocoon uses

All three screenshots use the same set of banners. The status banners always sit on the screen that is not showing the grid. The app dock always sits on the grid screen: at the top when the grid is on top, at the bottom when the grid is on the bottom.

Why the Miles window

Miles sits in the left third of the video, roughly columns 2–3 and the top two rows of the grid. Tiles go over the busy street on the right, which also calms the wallpaper down behind them.

Headers on the ledge

The header GIFs are 2×1 widgets in row three under Miles, so each page reads like a poster title. They use your red transparent headers.

Library spans two screens

Platforms on one, regex collections on the next. Pushing right from Platforms rolls straight into Collections, so it behaves like one half-and-half Library page.

Bottom-screen grid

The bottom screen only fits about four columns, so the grid scrolls with the focus like Cocoon does. The wallpaper is anchored left so Miles stays in frame.

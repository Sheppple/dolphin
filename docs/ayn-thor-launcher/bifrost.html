Bifrost

A top-screen launcher concept for the AYN Thor. Six columns, three rows, up to four pages. Tiles can span several cells. The bottom screen describes whatever the top screen is pointing at, and turns into the editor when you rearrange things.

Click the device once, then drive it with the keyboard. Tiles and the bottom screen also respond to clicks.

Four pages to start from

Tile kit

Every tile is one of three kinds. In edit mode each tile wears a size badge in its kind's colour, borrowed from the Thor's own face buttons.

AGames

  • Single game. Cover art with the console's case band. At 2×2 and up it switches to full artwork with a Play prompt.
  • Android game. Same tile, launched as an app. Shows with a green badge.

BFolders

  • Favourites. Anything starred with X.
  • Random. 12 games, reshuffled every morning.
  • Android games. Installed apps tagged as games.
  • Emulator. Everything one app runs, such as Dolphin or FinalBurn Neo.
  • Console. Can render as a full-bleed platform card.
  • Genre. Optionally limited to certain consoles.
  • Custom regex. Matched against titles, with a live match count while you type.
  • Never played. Under 30 minutes of playtime. A backlog shelf.

XWidgets Y

  • Clock. Clean, Mario HUD (the date reads as WORLD 10-9), or CRT.
  • Recently played. Strip of covers. Can be limited to a console.
  • Music player. Controls whatever Android is playing.
  • Last played. Big artwork. A resumes.
  • Random game. A rolls, A again plays. Skins: dice, ? block, coin slot.
  • Playtime. Last 7 days as bars, a coin counter, or arcade credits.
  • Image pin. Pure mood. Sets the theme of a page.
  • Android widget (green). Anything from the system widget picker, hosted in a tile.

More page ideas

Pages work best with one idea each: a franchise, a platform, a mood, or a job to do.

Spooky Season

For October only. Regex ghost|mansion|castlevania|resident, a Luigi's Mansion hero, and a dark wallpaper with a moon pin.

Handheld Heritage

A 6×1 strip of console folders in release order: Game Boy, Color, Advance, DS, 3DS. The best of each sits underneath.

Couch Night

Party, Sports and Fighting folders limited to 4-player consoles, plus a 2×2 random roll for "what are we playing".

Backlog

The Never played folder, a random roll that only picks from it, and a playtime widget set to this month.

Hyrule

Regex zelda|link, covers arranged by era from NES to 3DS, and a Wind Waker sea image pin.

Pocket Monsters

Regex pok[eé]mon across GBC, GBA and DS, with a pixel route image and a box-art strip.

PlayStation Shelf

A platform page in PS2 blue: PS1, PS2 and PSP folders, plus emulator folders for DuckStation and NetherSX2.

Commute

Games that suspend well (GBA, DS), a 3×1 music player, and a battery widget for the Thor and your earbuds.

Building it for real

How the prototype maps to Android, if this turns into an actual launcher.

  1. Home app on the top display. A launcher activity with the HOME category. If Android sees the Thor's bottom panel as a second display, the companion view can be a Presentation or a second activity started with setLaunchDisplayId.
  2. Android widgets. Host them with AppWidgetHost. Bind with bindAppWidgetIdIfAllowed and pass the tile's size in dp, so a 2×1 tile tells the widget how much room it has.
  3. Library and art. Scan ROM folders and reuse ES-DE's gamelist.xml and media folders, so covers, genres and playtime come along.
  4. Launching Dolphin. Start org.dolphinemu.dolphinemu/.ui.main.MainActivity with the game as a content URI in the intent data, or as a path in the AutoStartFile extra. Both are read by StartupHandler.kt in this repo's Android source.
  5. Playtime. Record a timestamp at launch and another when the launcher resumes. UsageStatsManager can fill gaps for Android games.
  6. Music tile. MediaSessionManager.getActiveSessions with notification-listener access gives title, art and transport controls.
  7. Layout file. One small JSON document per page, shown on the right for the page you're on. Folder rules are data, so a regex folder is a single line.

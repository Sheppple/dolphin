Bifrost, three ways

Three complete launcher designs for a black AYN Thor. Each one keeps a single wallpaper behind all four pages; it drifts a little as you change page. Pages are grouped by what you want to do, never by franchise.

Click a device, then: ←↑→↓ move AEnter open BEsc back XX favourite YY edit page QE L1 / R1

One wallpaper

The wallpaper is 15% wider than the screen and slides as you page, so the four pages read as one place. Empty cells are deliberate: they let the wallpaper show through.

Pages by purpose

Home for picking up where you left off, a mixed shelf of what you're playing, a library of folders, and a page for Android apps and widgets. Franchises live in folders, not pages.

Same engine underneath

All three use the same tiles, folders, widgets and editor. Only the wallpaper, tile shape, type and focus style change, so you could switch between them in settings.
